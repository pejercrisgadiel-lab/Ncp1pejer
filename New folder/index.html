AquaStat: Arduino-Based Water Quality Monitor

AquaStat is an Arduino-based water quality monitoring system engineered to provide accessible, real-time tracking of essential parameters like water level, pH, and turbidity. By leveraging affordable embedded microcontrollers for continuous data collection, the project directly advances UN SDG 6 (Clean Water and Sanitation) through proactive contamination awareness and efficient water resource management. At the same time, AquaStat embodies UN SDG 9 (Industry, Innovation, and Infrastructure) by demonstrating how cost-effective, scalable technology can innovate resilient environmental infrastructure for underserved areas.
